## Method notes

- Likert answers are coded ordinally; correlations are Spearman (rank-based); p-values corrected with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR.
- Burnout: 13 overwork items rescaled 0-100 (item 'energy for friends/family' reverse-scored); personal = items 1-6, work = items 7-13. Cut-offs 50/75 are conventional, editable at the top of the script.
- ProQOL-9: subscale means of three items each. UWES-3: mean of three items (1-7).
- Freelancer and organisational answer blocks are merged for shared scales; job-demand/resource, turnover and EAP items exist only for the organisational block.
- Correlation and regression show association, not causation; the sample is self-selected.
